TEMPERATURE SENSOR:
    The sensor should measure a new temperature every 30 seconds, cycling through
    a predefined sequence of temperature values.
    The measured temperature must be published as 'room_temperature' and printed
    to the console.

    room_temperatures = [22.0, 24.0, 26.0, 28.0, 30.0]

FAN:
    The fan should read the current room temperature and compare it with a
     predefined temperature threshold.
    
    If the temperature is above the threshold and the fan is powered on and
     the fan should rotate at the selected speed. 
    Otherwise, the fan should remain stopped.
